About

One of the central qualities of the awakened heart.

Metta

The concept of Metta in Buddhism is one of the central qualities of the awakened heart.

The word Metta comes from the Pali language and is usually translated as loving-kindness, benevolence, or unconditional friendliness.

It is a state of being in which one sincerely wishes for the wellbeing, safety, peace, and liberation of all beings.

Metta is the heartfelt wish for all beings to be happy, free from suffering, and able to flourish. It begins by opening our own hearts, with kindness, compassion, and mindful awareness, and allowing these qualities to naturally extend to others.

Through the Metta Collective, we are bringing together our unique gifts in the spirit of service, creating spaces where people feel safe to explore their inner world, meet life’s challenges with courage, and discover the possibility of healing and transformation. We hope to inspire one another, and our wider community, to loosen the boundaries that separate us, cultivate compassionate friendliness, and live with greater awareness. Together, we believe that through mindfulness, genuine human connection, and an open heart, our suffering can gradually be transformed into insight, wisdom, and a deeper capacity to care for ourselves, one another, and the world.

Vibhu

I believe that our greatest challenges can become our greatest teachers.

Life inevitably brings moments of loss, uncertainty, anxiety, grief and transition. While our instinct is often to move away from these experiences, I have come to see them as profound invitations, opportunities to pause, to listen deeply, and to discover that even in our darkest moments, the seeds of healing, wisdom and transformation are already present.

For over two decades, I have explored mindfulness, contemplative traditions, psychology, neuroscience and spiritual inquiry. Although each speaks in its own language, they all point towards the same truth: freedom is not found by escaping our experience, but by learning to meet it with awareness, courage and compassion.

Again and again, I have witnessed that when we stop resisting what is present and begin to understand it, something fundamental begins to shift. Fear gives way to clarity. Struggle softens into acceptance. What once felt like suffering can become the very ground from which resilience, peace and a more authentic way of living emerge.

I have come to believe that understanding is, in itself, transformative. This understanding continues to shape both my work and my life, reminding me that no matter where we find ourselves, there is always the possibility of meeting life with greater openness, deeper compassion and a freedom that comes not from changing our circumstances, but from transforming our relationship with them.

Corporate Wellbeing & Collective Flourishing

We partner with organisations to create workplaces where people and teams flourish through greater awareness, resilience, purpose and meaningful connection.

Our work integrates mindfulness, reflective practice and organisational development to cultivate healthier cultures, wiser leadership and more sustainable ways of working together.

The programme

A Foundation Programme for Collective Flourishing

Supporting Collective Flourishing is an experiential programme that helps leaders and teams build the foundations for healthier, more conscious ways of working together. Through mindfulness, reflective inquiry and practical workplace application, participants develop greater self-awareness, resilience and emotional intelligence while exploring how purpose, trust and meaningful connection contribute to both organisational wellbeing and sustainable performance.

Designed as an accessible introduction to our approach, the programme inspires meaningful conversations, strengthens workplace relationships and lays the foundation for deeper leadership and organisational development.

Offerings / 01 · With Vibhu

The Inner Journey of Parenting

The Inner Journey of Parenting is a transformative journey that invites parents to explore themselves, understand their children more deeply, and nurture more meaningful relationships.

Rather than focusing solely on managing children’s behaviour, the course explores how our beliefs, conditioning, emotional patterns, and life experiences shape the way we parent. It helps parents recognise their triggers, understand their children’s inner worlds, and respond with greater awareness, empathy, and emotional presence.

Through self-reflection, mindfulness, and practical relational tools, parents learn to move beyond reactive patterns and build deeper emotional connections with their children. The course also explores children’s emotional needs, communication, self-worth, and the importance of creating a secure and supportive environment for their growth.

By transforming the way we see ourselves, our children, and our relationship with them, we can move from control to connection and from managing behaviour to nurturing understanding and empowerment. Ultimately, it is an invitation to grow alongside your child and discover a more conscious, connected, and fulfilling experience of parenting.

Offerings / 02 · With Vibhu

Grief & Emotional Healing

Grief is not only about losing someone we love; it can also arise from the loss of a relationship, a significant life change, or the future we once imagined.

I offer a compassionate, supportive space to help you navigate grief, make sense of complex emotions, and reconnect with yourself during times of loss.

Through mindfulness, guided meditation, emotional awareness, self-inquiry, and therapeutic reflective practices, we gently explore what you are carrying, at a pace that feels right for you. Rather than rushing you towards acceptance or asking you to move on, the work is about developing the capacity to be with your emotions, meet yourself with greater compassion, and gradually loosen the patterns of pain, guilt, or inner struggle that may be adding to your suffering.

Together, we work towards greater emotional steadiness, inner clarity, and the ability to honour what has been lost while making room for life as it unfolds. You don’t have to have the right words or know where to begin; you only need a space where your experience can be met with care and understanding.

Private Transformational Work

One-to-One Deep Inquiry

This is a deeply personal journey for those who feel called to understand themselves more fully and create lasting change from within.

Through one-to-one dialogue, reflective inquiry and mindfulness-based practices, we explore the patterns, beliefs and conditioned responses that shape the way you experience yourself, your relationships and the world around you.

Rather than offering quick solutions or techniques to manage symptoms, this work invites a deeper understanding of the underlying causes of suffering. As awareness grows, new possibilities naturally emerge, bringing greater clarity, emotional freedom and a more authentic way of living.

Each journey is shaped by the individual, unfolding at a pace that honours your unique experiences, questions and aspirations.

Transformative Journeys

A Small Group Journey in Awareness and Self-Understanding

Transformative Journeys are intimate, small-group experiences designed for those who wish to explore the deeper dimensions of self-understanding within a supportive community.

Over three to four months, participants engage in a shared process of inquiry, reflection and mindful practice, discovering that many of the struggles we experience are part of our common human condition.

The journey offers the opportunity to slow down, observe familiar patterns with fresh awareness, and learn through both personal insight and the experiences of others. In a space built on trust, compassion and confidentiality, the group becomes a catalyst for reflection, connection and meaningful transformation.

Each journey unfolds organically, responding to the needs of the group while remaining grounded in the principles of awareness, compassion and deep inquiry. Rather than seeking to fix ourselves, we learn to meet our experience with curiosity, understanding and presence, creating the conditions for lasting change to emerge.
